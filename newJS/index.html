⚡ Fast Stock In / Stock Out Action

ℹ️ Movement & Audit Rules

Stock In:

  • Adds units to available inventory immediately.
  • Updates status from 'Out of Stock' or 'Low Stock' to 'In Stock' dynamically.
  • Generates an immutable entry in the audit trail.

Stock Out:

  • Validates that requested dispatch quantity does not exceed current on-hand stock.
  • If stock falls at or below minimum threshold, triggers low stock warning.
  • Records user identity, IP address, and signed negative delta.
All movements are permanently recorded with full audit trail compliance.
